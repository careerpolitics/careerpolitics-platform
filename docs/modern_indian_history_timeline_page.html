Modern Indian History (1600–1950)

Section-based flowchart timeline for easy reading on a custom Forem page.

Best template choice: use a normal HTML custom page (no page template) so this layout renders exactly as designed.

1) 1600–1707 · Entry & Early Trade

1600: EIC charter➜ 1612: Surat foothold➜ Madras, Bombay, Calcutta bases
  • English East India Company enters as a trading corporation.
  • Factories and ports become strategic coastal anchors.

2) 1707–1765 · From Trade to Political Power

1707: Mughal decline➜ 1757: Plassey➜ 1764: Buxar➜ 1765: Diwani rights
  • Company shifts from commerce to territorial control.
  • Bengal revenue strengthens military and administrative expansion.

3) 1765–1818 · Company State Formation

Revenue control in Bengal➜ Mysore wars➜ Maratha defeat (1818)
  • British supremacy expands beyond Bengal into major regions.
  • Indian polities are restructured through alliance and warfare.

4) 1818–1857 · Reform, Annexation, Discontent

Legal/education reforms➜ Doctrine of Lapse➜ Annexations increase
  • Social and educational reforms reshape elite politics.
  • Policies and annexations deepen military and civil resentment.

5) 1857–1885 · Revolt and Crown Raj

1857: Revolt➜ 1858: Crown rule➜ Administrative centralization
  • Company rule ends; governance moves to the British Crown.
  • Army reorganization and bureaucratic control intensify.

6) 1885–1919 · Organized Nationalism

1885: INC founded➜ 1905: Bengal partition➜ Swadeshi movement➜ 1919: Jallianwala Bagh
  • Congress evolves from petitions to broader political mobilization.
  • Swadeshi links economics, culture, and anti-colonial protest.

7) 1919–1935 · Gandhian Mass Movements

1920: Non-Cooperation➜ 1930: Salt March➜ Civil Disobedience➜ 1935: Govt of India Act
  • National movement gains deep rural and urban participation.
  • Constitutional reforms open limited provincial autonomy.

8) 1935–1950 · Decolonization to Republic

1942: Quit India➜ 1947: Independence & Partition➜ 1949: Constitution adopted➜ 1950: Republic begins
  • War-era pressures and negotiations accelerate imperial withdrawal.
  • State integration and constitutional democracy shape modern India.
